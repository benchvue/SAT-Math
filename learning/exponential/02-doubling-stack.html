Doubling vs adding

Both stacks start with 1 block. One adds 2 blocks each step, the other doubles. Slide the step and watch which one runs away.

Side by side

x2x + 1change2xchange

What to notice

  • The blue stack grows by the same amount (+2) every step. That is linear.
  • The green stack grows by the same factor (×2) every step. That is exponential.
  • At first adding is ahead (3 vs 2 at x = 1), but by x = 3 doubling has caught up and from then on it pulls away.
  • SAT tip: “increases by the same number” → linear. “increases by the same percent or factor” → exponential.
