Three functions start together near the origin. Move x to the right and find out which one wins in the long run.

The big idea: 2x and x² tie at x = 2 and x = 4. Between them x² is ahead, but after x = 4 the exponential wins forever. Any growing exponential eventually beats any linear or quadratic function.
