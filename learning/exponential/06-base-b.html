The base b: growth or decay

In y = bx, the base is what you multiply by each step. Bigger than 1 grows, between 0 and 1 shrinks.

Read the base like the SAT does

  • b > 1: growth. Each step multiplies by b, an increase of (b − 1) × 100%. Example: 1.25 means +25% per step.
  • 0 < b < 1: decay. Each step keeps b of the value, a decrease of (1 − b) × 100%. Example: 0.8 means −20% per step.
  • b = 1: nothing changes; the graph is the flat line y = 1.
  • Every curve y = bx passes through (0, 1), and the point at x = 1 is (1, b).
  • (12)x is the mirror image of 2x across the y-axis.
